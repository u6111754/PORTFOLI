About me

Hello, my name is Cèlia and I'm a 22 year-old photographer from Tarragona. From a very young age, I have felt a special sensitivity and interest in art.

I am specialized in photography and visual arts. My work focuses primarily on portraits, black-and-white and analog photography in its various formats. I am deeply drawn to analog photography, as it reminds me of our roots and provides a physical medium through wich an image can exist.

I am highly observant, detail-oriented, and deeply passionate about the visual arts. For this reason, I strive to explore various fields within the visual arts, innovate, and develop my skils through new techniques and different ways of capturing the essence of what i want te express.
